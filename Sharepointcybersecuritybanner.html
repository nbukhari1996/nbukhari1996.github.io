Cybersecurity — what to actually watch for

Before you click

  • Hover over links first. Unfamiliar URL? Don't click — ask IT.
  • Check the sender's actual email address, not just the name shown.
  • Urgent payment request or "account locked" email? Call the person to confirm before acting.

Passwords

  • IT will never ask for your password. Ever. If someone does, it's not IT.
  • Don't reuse your work password anywhere else.
  • Unexpected login/MFA prompt? Deny it, change your password, tell IT.

If something looks off

  • Forward it to security@esadservices.com — 10 seconds, no penalty for false alarms.
  • Don't wait to check yourself first. Report, then IT verifies.
